\section{Conclusion and Perspectives}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\subsection{Summary of Contributions}
\label{subsec:conclusion-contributions}
\rmq{State the contributions at the level they were actually made. The individual
architectures are standard and are not claimed. What was built is the layer above them: a
shared output contract across organs, calibrated confidence carried from perception through
to reasoning, an explicit representation of absent evidence and model scope, and a
deterministic escalation policy independent of the language model. Add the methodological
contribution: case-level evaluation established for three datasets that did not provide it.}

\subsection{Achieved Objectives}
\label{subsec:conclusion-objectives}
\rmq{Return to the Business and Data Science Objectives of Section~\ref{subsec:objectives}
and assess each against the evidence. Mark partially achieved objectives as partial. An
honest assessment here is more persuasive than a complete one, because the limitations
chapter immediately precedes it and the jury has already read it.}

\subsection{Current Limitations}
\label{subsec:conclusion-limitations}
\rmq{Summarise Section~\ref{subsec:results-limitations} in a few sentences; do not repeat
it. The single most important sentence: this system has not been validated against clinical
outcomes and is not a diagnostic device.}

\subsection{Difficulties Encountered}
\label{subsec:conclusion-difficulties}
\rmq{The genuine obstacles and what each forced. Intermittent GPU availability, which made
each training run expensive enough to justify estimating candidate changes analytically
first; datasets whose statistical unit was undocumented; taxonomies that did not match the
images; and hypotheses that had to be abandoned when measurement contradicted them. Frame
each as a constraint that shaped the method, which is what they were.}

\subsection{Future Work}
\label{subsec:conclusion-future}

\subsubsection{Additional organs}
\rmq{The vascular / DVT module deliberately excluded from this internship, and the FAST
examination. Both fit the existing contract without changes to it.}

\subsubsection{Video analysis}
\rmq{All imaging modules operate on frames. Lung sliding and cardiac wall motion are
temporal signs that a frame-based model cannot represent at all, which bounds what the
current system can detect regardless of how much data it is given.}

\subsubsection{Improved calibration and external validation}
\rmq{Larger case-level datasets, prospective data collection, and validation on a cohort
from a different institution --- the evidence the current results most lack.}

\subsubsection{Simulation and the digital twin}
\rmq{The escalation route currently ends at a recommendation to examine further. The
intended continuation is a simulation environment in which a candidate decision can be
explored before it is committed to. Present this as a perspective, and be explicit that no
part of it was built during this internship.}

\subsubsection{Clinical evaluation}
\rmq{The step that would make the system meaningful: evaluation with clinicians, measuring
whether the assistant changes time-to-diagnosis or error rate rather than whether its
individual models score well.}
