\paragraph{Recovering case identity for the lung dataset.} The release carries no patient
identifier linking clips belonging to one patient, so case identity was reconstructed from the
filename pattern, which encodes the source acquisition. Clips sharing that prefix are treated
as one case and never separated across folds. This is a heuristic: it can merge two patients
whose files happen to share a prefix, or split one patient recorded under two. The residual
uncertainty is stated as a limitation in Section~\ref{subsec:results-limitations} and is the
reason the lung case count is reported as approximate rather than exact.

\paragraph{Class definitions.} The gallbladder taxonomy trains on eight classes ---
gallstones, cholecystitis, membranous/gangrenous, perforation, polyps, adenomyomatosis,
carcinoma, wall thickening --- and reports five by summing group probabilities:
cholelithiasis; acute cholecystitis (any severity), formed from cholecystitis, membranous and
perforation; polyps and adenomyomatosis; carcinoma; and wall thickening. A ninth folder present
in the original release was excluded after the per-case inspection described in
Section~\ref{subsec:gallbladder-module}.

The lung module models four findings: B-lines, consolidation, pleural effusion and pleural
thickening. Pneumothorax is not modelled --- the dataset contains no positive cases.

\paragraph{Licences.} Conditions of use are stated per dataset in Section~\ref{subsec:data-overview}.

\subsection{Confusion Matrices}
\label{app:confusion}

\paragraph{Gallbladder, five reported classes.} Per-class recall is given in
Table~\ref{tab:gb-results}. The structure of the errors matters more than the diagonal:
\textbf{18\% of the fine-grained model's confusions stay inside their reported group}, meaning
82\% cross a group boundary. That figure is what decided against reporting the coarser
three-category grouping as the module's output --- if the groups captured a distinction the
model could see, confusions would concentrate within them, and they do not.

\paragraph{The collapsed matrix.} The taxonomy selection of
Section~\ref{subsec:gallbladder-module} operated on the fine-grained confusion matrix directly:
for each candidate grouping, the matrix rows and columns belonging to one group were summed to
give the confusion matrix that grouping would produce, and balanced accuracy was read off
without training anything. The chosen candidate was predicted at 63.0\% and measured at 59.7\%
after training.

\paragraph{Triage.} The three-tier confusion is summarised by
Table~\ref{tab:app-triage}. The error that matters is asymmetric and is reported separately in
Section~\ref{subsec:triage-error}: dangerous under-triage, a high-acuity case predicted low,
occurred at 3.5\% without the safety threshold and 1.5\% with it.

\subsection{Example Grad-CAM Maps}
\label{app:gradcam}

Attention maps are produced by each imaging module and the aggregate statistics are reported in
Section~\ref{subsec:explainability}.

The selection principle for any figures placed here matters more than the figures themselves: a
selection of successful maps is not evidence, because a viewer who knows where the pathology
lies will read the map as pointing at it. Any maps reproduced should therefore include cases the
module got wrong, so that a reader can see whether the attention differs between correct and
incorrect predictions --- which is the only comparison from which anything can be concluded
without expert annotation.

The measured statistics stand on their own regardless: centroid spread, area above
half-maximum and normalised entropy were computed over the full sets rather than over a chosen
subset, together with an explicit test for centre bias.

\paragraph{Deployment.} The application is an HTTP service written with FastAPI and served
by uvicorn on port 8501, delivering a generated HTML interface whose every displayed value
is computed by the pipeline described in Section~\ref{sec:integration}. The port is the one
Streamlit uses by default, which is an artefact of history rather than of design: the first
prototype was a Streamlit application, and the port number was kept when the interface was
rebuilt so that existing links continued to work. That prototype is retained under
\texttt{\_archive/} as a record of the earlier iteration and forms no part of the deployed
system.

\paragraph{Environment.} Python 3.13, PyTorch 2.13, torchvision 0.28, scikit-learn 1.5,
XGBoost 3.2, NumPy 2.3. Segmentation uses \texttt{segmentation\_models\_pytorch}; the language
model runs through \texttt{llama-cpp-python} with a CUDA build. Training and inference ran on
Google Colab T4 GPUs.

Dependencies are pinned in three files rather than one, because the sets genuinely differ.
\texttt{requirements.txt} holds what the running service needs and is what a container
installs; \texttt{requirements-dev.txt} adds the test suite, the linter and the two security
scanners; \texttt{requirements-train.txt} adds training and experiment tracking. The division
is not cosmetic: PyTorch is pinned to its CPU build, because the deployed pipeline runs on CPU
by design, and a scanner or a tracking client inside a production image would be surface that
performs no work there.

\paragraph{Reproducing the training runs.} Each module has one notebook under
\texttt{notebooks/}, which loads its dataset, performs its own grouped split, trains, evaluates
and exports both weights and calibrator. The notebooks are self-contained and were the interface
through which every reported figure was produced.

\paragraph{Reproducing the safety benchmark.} The 322 tests require no GPU, no model weights and
no dataset:

\begin{verbatim}
python -m src.agents.tests.run_benchmark
\end{verbatim}

Test modules are discovered from the directory rather than listed, after a hand-maintained list
silently omitted a new file of fourteen tests and the suite reported the same total as before,
all passing.

\paragraph{Reproducing the reasoning evaluation.} \texttt{run\_case} executes one scenario end
to end. Its \texttt{-{}-dry-run} mode prints the clinical state, the escalation decision and the
exact prompt without loading the language model:

\begin{verbatim}
python -m src.agents.clinical.run_case --scenario conflict --dry-run
python -m src.agents.clinical.run_case --scenario conflict --n-gpu-layers -1
\end{verbatim}

Determinism is enforced by temperature 0, a fixed seed and a KV-cache reset before each call;
two identical calls produce byte-identical output.

\paragraph{The output schema.} A report carries the organ, a status, a list of detected findings
with calibrated confidences, a list of findings screened for and not detected, and a reliability
block declaring scope. Section~\ref{subsec:output-contract} states the four validation rules and
the reason for each.

\subsection{Finding and Choosing the Data}
\label{app:data-search}

The single largest cost of this project was not training models. It was deciding what to train
them on. This appendix records that work, because the report as a whole shows the datasets that
were chosen and says little about the ones that were not, and because the difficulty was
methodological rather than clerical: at no point was the obstacle a shortage of files.

\subsubsection{The constraint that shaped everything}

The subject requires point-of-care ultrasound from an emergency department, labelled with
clinical findings, linked to the patient's physiological state, and usable outside the
institution that produced it. No such dataset exists publicly. Every candidate satisfies some
of those conditions and fails at least one, and the project's architecture is in large part a
response to that fact rather than a design chosen in the abstract.

The multi-agent structure --- separate perception modules feeding a clinical state, with the
reasoning layer reading that state rather than the images --- is the direct consequence. No
single source carried images \emph{and} vitals \emph{and} laboratory values for the same
patient, so a model that consumed all three jointly could not have been trained on anything.
Modelling each modality separately and combining them in a structured state was the only
option available, and it turned out to be the safer architecture as well: the escalation logic
that runs before any model can be tested independently precisely because it takes structured
inputs rather than pixels.

\subsubsection{What was searched, and what was rejected}

Candidate sources were assessed against four criteria: whether the labels were clinical
findings rather than diagnostic categories, whether the case identity was recoverable, whether
the licence permitted use and redistribution of derived work, and whether the acquisition
setting resembled emergency point-of-care use.

--- which is the behaviour the whole project argues for, applied to its own gaps.

\subsubsection{The Iran ED vital signs, and why apparent richness was not richness}
\label{app:data-search-iran}

The most instructive rejection concerns the triage data. The Iran ED dataset (143{,}582 visits,
2017--2022) carries a full set of vital signs, and including them raised accuracy to
approximately 75\%. The result did not survive inspection: accuracy was 98\% on Iran rows and
62\% on NHAMCS rows.

The cause was in the recording pattern rather than in the model. Every vital-sign field is
more than 90\% populated for exactly one triage grade and less than 5\% populated for all
others. That is not physiological missingness --- an emergency department takes vitals on
resuscitation patients too --- but an artefact of one hospital's documentation workflow. The
model had learned \emph{whether a field was recorded} as a proxy for the label, which is a
shortcut that cannot transfer anywhere and would fail silently in use.

Only age, sex and arrival mode were kept from that source. The consequence is stated in the
results as a bound rather than buried: the triage model operates largely on demographics and
presenting complaint, and roughly 88\% of every vital sign is missing in the pooled training
data. That is also the origin of the missing-data behaviour the system is built around; the
architecture treats absence as a first-class state because the data forced the question early.

\subsubsection{Direction verified rather than assumed}

The Iran dataset's triage grade does not state its direction, and the source paper is
paywalled. Rather than assume the common convention, the direction was established from the
data: mean age falls monotonically from 58 to 34 across grades~1 to~5, and critical-status
assessments concentrate in grades~1--2, both consistent with grade~1 being most urgent. Had
the assumption been made the wrong way round, every downstream result would have been
inverted while remaining internally consistent, and nothing in the training process would have
signalled it.

\subsubsection{Licensing, and what it prevents}

The imaging sources are released under research licences, several non-commercial
(CC~BY--NC~4.0), and none permits redistribution of the media. This is why the repository
contains manifests rather than data, why reproduction requires the reader to obtain the
sources themselves, and why the MLOps dataset versioning hashes the manifest rather than the
files (Section~\ref{subsec:mlops}). A constraint of this kind is easy to discover late and
expensive to discover late; it was checked before the datasets were adopted.

\subsubsection{What the search cost, and what it produced}

The most valuable outcome of this phase was a definition rather than a dataset: the decision
that the unit of analysis is the \emph{case}, not the file. Applying it reduced the gallbladder
set from 10{,}694 images to 220 cases and the lung set from 187 clips to 165 groups, and
lowered every metric reported afterwards. Chapter~\ref{sec:data} records that work; this
appendix records that it came out of the search rather than out of the modelling.

Two numbers give the scale of what that leaves. Pleural effusion has 24 positive clips in the
lung evaluation cohort, so a single clip changing class is 4.2 percentage points of recall ---
the limit that the promotion gate ran into in Section~\ref{subsec:results-mlops}. Small data
is not a caveat appended to the results here; it is the condition the entire system was
designed around.
